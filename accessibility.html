Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 2026

HomeNetIP (erezults) is committed to making this website accessible to people with disabilities, in accordance with Israeli Standard 5568 (based on WCAG 2.0, Level AA) and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations.

What we’ve done

Semantic HTML structure and headings, descriptive link text, alt text on all informative images, a minimum 4.5:1 color-contrast ratio for text, full keyboard operability with visible focus indicators, a “skip to content” link on every page, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

Accessibility widget

An accessibility widget is available in the bottom corner of every page (keyboard- and screen-reader-operable) offering larger text, a high-contrast mode, and an option to stop animations. Your preference is saved only in your browser’s local storage.

Known limitations

This statement reflects our ongoing efforts. If you encounter any content or feature that is not accessible to you, please let us know using the contact details below and we will do our best to address it.

Accessibility coordinator

Erez Cohen (erezults) — homenetip.support@erezults.com

We aim to respond to accessibility requests within a reasonable time and no later than required by law.
