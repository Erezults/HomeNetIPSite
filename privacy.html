Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

HomeNetIP is a local-first app. Your data — devices, rooms, categories, images and settings — is stored on your device and synced only through your own private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). There is no sign-up and no login with us.

What we collect

Nothing. HomeNetIP has no servers, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. We never see, transmit, or store your data. Network scanning — and all insights and analysis — run entirely on your device over your local network and are never sent anywhere.

Cookies & local storage

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels. The optional accessibility widget (bottom corner) saves your text size / contrast / motion preference in your browser’s local storage only — it stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

iCloud

Sync uses your private iCloud database, governed by Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy. Your data is not shared with us or with other users.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details — only Apple’s confirmation that the purchase is valid.

Permissions

Local Network (to discover and wake devices), Camera/Photos (optional, for device images), and Face ID (optional, to lock the app). Each is used only for the stated feature and only on your device.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your local data and your iCloud copy.
